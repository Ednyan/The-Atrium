Privacy Policy — The Atrium

Last updated: 2026-07-27. Contact: thedigitalatrium@gmail.com

The Atrium ("the app", "we", "us") is a collaborative canvas application developed by mindeformer. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can control or remove it.

Who this applies to

The Atrium is available as a web app and as a desktop app. Some data handling differs between the two — this is noted below wherever it matters.

Account data

On the web app, creating an account requires an email address, a username, and a password. This is handled by our backend provider, Supabase, which stores and encrypts your credentials — we do not see or store your raw password ourselves.

On the desktop app, no account or sign-in is required by default: your username and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Content you create

Anything you place on a canvas — text, images, shapes, embedded links, audio, or video — is stored so it can be shown to you (and to other collaborators, if the atrium/lobby you're in allows editing or is shared) the next time it's opened. On the web app this is stored in our backend database and file storage. On the desktop app this is stored locally in a database file on your device, and only leaves your device if you explicitly use a feature that uploads or shares it.

Presence data

While you're active in a shared atrium, your approximate cursor position, chosen display color, and online status are broadcast in real time to other people currently in that same atrium, so collaboration feels live. This is not stored permanently — it exists only while you're connected.

Cookies and local storage

The Atrium does not use tracking or advertising cookies. On the web app, your browser's local storage (not cookies) is used only for things the app needs to function:

  • Keeping you signed in between visits (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Remembering interface preferences — e.g. which atrium you were last in, your chosen player color, zoom sensitivity, undo history depth, and similar display toggles.

None of this is used to track you across other sites, build an advertising profile, or shared with any third party — so no cookie-consent banner is shown. You're welcome to clear your browser's local storage for this site at any time; it simply signs you out and resets these preferences to their defaults.

Pinterest integration (optional)

If you choose to connect a Pinterest account (Profile Settings → Connect Pinterest), you'll be sent to Pinterest's own sign-in and consent screen. We never see your Pinterest password.

  • We request read-only access to your boards and pins, and your Pinterest username, solely so you can choose a board and import its pins as images onto your own canvas.
  • The access token Pinterest gives us is stored server-side and is never exposed to other users of the app, including collaborators in a shared atrium.
  • We do not post, modify, or delete anything on your Pinterest account — access is read-only.
  • We do not use your Pinterest data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties beyond what's needed to make the requested API calls to Pinterest itself.
  • You can disconnect at any time from Profile Settings, which deletes your stored Pinterest tokens immediately. Pins you've already imported remain on your canvas as ordinary content (see "Content you create" above) unless you delete them yourself.

Other third-party services we use

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage, and realtime messaging for the web app.
  • Cloudflare Pages — hosts the web app.
  • Pinterest API — only used for accounts that explicitly connect Pinterest, as described above.

We do not run any advertising or analytics/tracking scripts on this site.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete individual pieces of content yourself at any time from within the app. On the web app, you can also delete your entire account yourself, immediately, from Profile Settings → Delete Account — this removes your profile, every atrium you own, and any connected Pinterest tokens. Content you placed in atriums owned by other people is not deleted, since removing it could take content other people are relying on out of their shared atrium — instead it's anonymized so it's no longer linked to you or your name. This cannot be undone. If you'd rather we do it for you, or you're on desktop, contact thedigitalatrium@gmail.com and we'll process the request promptly.

Security

Access to your data is scoped per account and, for shared atriums, per the permissions the atrium's owner configures (who can view, edit, or is blocked). Sensitive credentials (passwords, Pinterest tokens) are never exposed to other users through the app's normal features.

Administrator access

I operate this service, which means I hold the keys to the database it runs on. In practice that is true of anyone running any hosted app, but I would rather state it plainly than leave you to assume otherwise.

My account can open any atrium, including private and password-protected ones, without being whitelisted or knowing the password. I may do this to investigate a bug you report, to look into content reported as abusive or illegal, or to recover data after something goes wrong. When I open an atrium I would not otherwise have access to, I do so invisibly — I will not appear in the list of people present and my cursor will not be shown. This is so that looking into a report doesn't announce itself to whoever is being reported.

This access is read-only. The administrator privilege does not allow editing, adding, or deleting anything in an atrium I don't own or haven't been invited to.

I don't browse people's private atriums out of curiosity, and I'm not doing anything automated with their contents — no analysis, no training data, no sharing with anyone else. If you would rather your references never sit on someone else's server at all, the desktop app stores everything locally on your own device and this section doesn't apply to it.

This app is operated by an individual developer, not a company. If you have questions, concerns, or a data request, the fastest way to reach me is thedigitalatrium@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

The Atrium is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.
